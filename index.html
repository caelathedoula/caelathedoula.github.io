Hi there! I’m Caela the Doula.

I’m so glad you’re here!

I am a birth and postpartum doula. Becoming a birth worker has been in my heart since the birth of my 3lb 8oz fighter, 20 years ago. I created HER birth doula services to help educate woman during pregnancy and beyond.

At HER Birth Doula services, I believe in having “your birth your way”. “Your birth your way” speaks to the power that you have within and the power that comes from being informed. I want to help women take back their power and own their birthing experiences. Birth is a natural process that should be honored not altered. As your Doula, I’m here to remind you of that very thing.

My goal is to provide the resources and support needed to help families make the best decisions for their birthing experiences. I am also passionate about making doula services available to teen mothers that may not otherwise receive the support that they need.
